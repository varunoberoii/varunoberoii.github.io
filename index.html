About Me

I'm a startup enthusiast and strategy consultant based in India. My journey started as the President of DTU’s Entrepreneurship Cell, where I helped budding campus founders bring their ideas to life.

Since then, while working at BCG and pursuing my MBA from IIM Lucknow, I’ve continued to consult student-led and early-stage startups — pro bono — across strategy, growth, collaborations, and investor readiness.

I believe student startups are scrappy, creative, and full of potential — they just need the right nudge. This is my way of giving back to the ecosystem that shaped me.
